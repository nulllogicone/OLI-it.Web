# OLI-it

## Mission

OLI-it is an open protocol for exchanging meaning, not just data.

It enables humans and intelligent agents to communicate through shared semantics.

OLI-it belongs to no single company, government, or person. It is public infrastructure, like HTTP, meant to be simple, decentralized, and freely implementable.

Its purpose is to augment human communication, preserve knowledge, and allow ideas to flow across systems and generations without losing intent.
